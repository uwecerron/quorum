## 3. Objection handling

**"Why not just do our own content?"**
You don't have the Guild's 6,250-member distribution or a neutral third-party voice. A rating is credible *because* it isn't the vendor's own marketing. You get the reach and the neutrality; you keep the attribution.

**"How do we know it drives consumption?"**
Every score and scorecard is a GoldRush query, tracked and reported. The Radar is a consumption funnel by construction — that's the point, and it's measurable.

**"$12k is a lot for a leaderboard."**
It's a quarter of exclusive placement + lead-gen + a live data showcase, against a $15–50k booth for three days. And it rides a live news moment; the attention window is now.

**"Is it live / real?"**
The scoring engine runs on GoldRush today (Compound, Uniswap, ENS score in real time). Launch is a `npm run` away. Happy to show a live score on a call.

**"Can we make it exclusive / co-owned?"**
Category exclusivity is included. Full co-branding ("GoldRush Governance Risk Radar") is available at the annual tier.
